I build practical real-time human sensing applications for consumer devices. At Northwestern, I focused on enabling smartwatches, AR glasses, and XR headsets to sense pose, body trajectory, and activity on-device, using sensors already built in (e.g., IMUs, microphones). In my PhD, I am expanding these approaches in three directions: context-aware interaction with AI agents, just-in-time interventions for diverse real-world applications, and novel health sensing methods on consumer devices.
